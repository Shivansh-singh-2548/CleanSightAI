📍 Select the Exact Waste Location

You can use GPS to detect your current position, search for a landmark, or click directly on the map.

How to select the exact point:
  1. Click "Use My Current Location" or search for a landmark.
  2. Wait for the map to zoom into the area.
  3. Click directly on the actual waste collection point.
  4. Check the coordinates displayed below the map.

About the Project

Building Cleaner Communities

CleanSight AI is a waste-reporting prototype that combines image-based AI predictions with location information. Its structured reports can help communities monitor reported waste problems and identify areas that may need attention.
